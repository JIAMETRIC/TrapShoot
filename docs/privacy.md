# TrapShoot — Privacy policy

*Effective 22 October 2026 · applies to TrapShoot (TrapShooterPro) 8.0 and later for iPhone and iPad*

**TrapShoot collects no data.** There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.

- **Rounds, settings and round audio** stay on your iPhone or iPad. They leave it only when you share or export a file
  yourself (scorecard, CSV, backup, round audio, diagnostics).
- **Microphone**: used only while the shot timer runs, to time your call and shots. Audio is processed on the device.
  It is saved only if you turn on Round audio, as a file in the app's folder that you can see in the Files app.
- **Location**: used only when you tap Weather, or when automatic weather fill is on and a new round is opened. The
  latitude and longitude, rounded to about 10 m, are sent once to Open‑Meteo (open-meteo.com) to get the current
  temperature, humidity, sky and wind. Nothing else is sent, and the location is not stored.
- **Notifications**: one optional local reminder for an unfinished round, created on the device. No push service.
- **Diagnostics**: Settings → About → Send diagnostics makes a text file with app and device facts and recent log
  lines, never your rounds. It is sent only if you choose to mail or share it.
- **Children**: the app collects nothing from anyone.
